How to combine the segment reports:
1. The reports are in time order. Use that order to reason about sequence, duration and
   change across the whole video.
2. For "how many times" questions, count the individual occurrences listed across ALL
   segments. Sum them; do not take any single segment's count as the total. Watch for the
   same continuous action being reported at the boundary between two adjacent segments —
   that is one occurrence, not two.
3. A segment that says "NOTHING RELEVANT IN THIS SEGMENT" is evidence of absence in that
   time range, not missing data.
4. For on-screen text, formulas, readings or chart values, use the values quoted in the
   reports verbatim.

Requirements:
1. Reason briefly (at most 5 lines), citing the timestamps you relied on.
2. On the second-to-last line, output the time interval of your supporting evidence,
   in exactly this format:  Evidence: MM:SS-MM:SS
3. On the last line, output your choice, in exactly this format:  Answer: X
   (where X is the option letter)
4. Some questions contain an abstention option ("N/A", "none of the above",
   "not available", "cannot be determined"). Treat it as a last resort. Pick it only
   when the reports positively rule out every concrete option. Sparse sampling is NOT a
   reason to abstain: the frames were a uniform sample of the video, so a detail that
   fell between two frames may still be the right answer — abstaining because the
   sampling was too coarse is the single most common way to lose this question.

Answer from the reports. If they are not fully conclusive, still choose the most likely
option and set the evidence interval to the time span you consider most relevant. Do not
use an abstention option to express low confidence.
